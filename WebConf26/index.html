About

Shaping the future of multimodal content safety.

The fourth MM4SG workshop brought together researchers in natural language processing, machine learning, computational social science, and ethics to exchange ideas on effective and responsible multimodal content analysis.

Call for papers

Topics

Hate, trolling, cyberbullying, scams, and abuse detection
Fake news, misinformation, rumor, and event detection
Multimodal sentiment analysis
Disaster response and crisis management
Multimodal healthcare applications using web data
Content analysis for sustainable development goals
Datasets, generation, foundation models, and LLMs
Fairness, bias, accountability, and transparency

Submission format

Double-blind ACM-format submissions were accepted as long papers (8 content pages) or short papers (4 content pages), with unlimited references and an optional appendix. The 2026 submission cycle is closed.

Important dates

Submission: January 18, 2026 AoE · Fast track: January 23 · Notification: January 25 · Camera-ready: February 2.
