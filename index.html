Why MM4SG

Multimodal systems shape what people see, believe, and share.

Images, video, text, audio, and platform signals now travel together. Their combination creates new opportunities for public benefit—and difficult questions around misinformation, abuse, bias, privacy, safety, and accountability.

Recent advances in large language and multimodal foundation models also make realistic fabricated content easier to create, placing new pressure on the authenticity and credibility of digital platforms.

MM4SG brings together researchers from natural language processing, computer vision, machine learning, computational social science, human-centered computing, and ethics to exchange ideas, share cutting-edge research, and develop robust content-analysis techniques.

Tentative call for papers

Call for Papers

We invite original contributions on a wide range of topics related to multimodal content analysis for social good, including but not limited to:

  • Hate, troll, cyberbullying, scams, and abuse detection
  • Fake news, misinformation, rumor, and event detection
  • Multimodal sentiment analysis
  • Disaster response and crisis management on the Web
  • Multimodal healthcare using Web data
  • Multimodal content analysis for sustainable development goals (SDGs)
  • New datasets for multimodal content analysis on the Internet
  • Large language models for multimodal content analysis on the Internet
  • Foundation models for multimodal content analysis on the Internet
  • Socially responsible multimodal content analysis: fairness, bias, accountability, and transparency
  • LLM alignment for multimodal content analysis
  • Aligning multimodal foundation models with human values and societal goals
  • Collaborative multimodal data sharing and replicability in vulnerable and data-poor contexts
  • Computational social science for understanding global challenges, including migration, disease spread, and civic discourse
